What Is This & How It Works
Why this tool exists, and how to get the most out of it.
Why this exists
Asking AI a vague question is like texting someone "help me" with no explanation — it wants to help, it just can't. This tool helps you give AI exactly what it needs to respond the way you actually need it to.
How to use it
01
Pick your mode
Choose the context that fits you — Agency, Education, Student, or Creative. Each mode surfaces the fields that actually matter for your situation.
02
Click chips or type your own
Select pre-built options with one click, or write a custom sentence in the text field below each section. Mix and match — both inputs combine into the final prompt. Skip if the section doesn't apply.
03
Copy and paste into any AI
Your structured prompt builds in real time on the right. Hit Copy and paste it directly into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — any AI you use.
