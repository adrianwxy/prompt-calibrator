What Is This & How It Works
Why this tool exists, and how to get the most out of it.
Why this exists
Asking AI a vague question is like texting someone "help me" with no explanation — it wants to help, it just can't. This tool helps you give AI exactly what it needs to respond the way you actually need it to.
How to use it
01
Pick your mode
Choose the context that fits you — Consulting, Education, Student, or Creative. Each mode surfaces the fields that actually matter for your situation.
02
Click chips or type your own
Select pre-built options with one click, or write a custom sentence in the text field below each section. Mix and match — both inputs combine into the final prompt. Skip if the section doesn't apply.
03
Copy and paste into any AI
Your structured prompt builds in real time on the right. Hit Copy and paste it directly into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — any AI you use.

The idea behind Prompt Calibrator
What makes this tool different — and why it was built this way.
01
Think before you ask
Most people open AI and type whatever comes to mind.
This tool slows that down — on purpose.
A few simple fields help you clarify what you actually want.
02
Prompts are situational
A teacher, a consultant, and a student don't need the same prompt.
Each mode highlights the fields that matter for that situation — instead of giving everyone a generic template.
03
AI shouldn't write your prompt
Many tools use AI to generate prompts for you.
Prompt Calibrator doesn't.
AI should answer your prompt — not write it. Your thinking stays in the loop.
04
Private by design
Everything runs in your browser.
No login. No storage. No tracking of what you write.
Your ideas and prompts stay on your device.
05
Build prompt literacy
The goal isn't just one good prompt.
It's learning how to structure your thinking before talking to AI — until it becomes a habit.
Data & Privacy
How this tool handles your information.
01
Your prompts never leave your browser
All prompt generation runs entirely client-side. Nothing you type is stored, transmitted, or collected — by design.
02
No account, no sign-up, anonymous use
There is no login, no profile, and no way to identify you. You are anonymous by design.
03
Anonymous usage analytics
This tool uses Google Analytics to understand general usage patterns — no personal data, no cross-site tracking.
04
Optional feedback form
The feedback form is entirely optional and powered by Google Forms. No email required.
This tool is built to respect your privacy, and will stay that way.
